## One-off assessments

| Situation                                                    | Outcome                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You cancel before the assessment starts                      | Full refund                                                                                                                                          |
| The assessment fails for our reasons and cannot be completed | Full refund                                                                                                                                          |
| We refuse the assessment under the Acceptable Use Policy     | Full refund of the assessment fee                                                                                                                    |
| The assessment completes and you dislike the score           | **No refund.** The score is the product. A refund for an unwelcome result would be a refund for honesty, and would make every other score worth less |
| The assessment completes but a finding was wrong             | No refund; the Appeals & Corrections Policy corrects the report at no charge                                                                         |

A completed assessment includes one free re-test within 30 days.

## Subscriptions

Cancel at any time from your console. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing period; we do not pro-rate part-months, and we do not charge a cancellation fee.

**What happens to your badge:** it expires at the end of the paid period and then renders as
expired wherever it is displayed. It is not revoked — you earned it — but it is not maintained
either, because a badge whose monitoring has stopped is a stale stamp, and a stale stamp is a
liability for everyone who sees it.

Your reports and history remain readable for the life of your account.

## Annual plans

Refundable pro rata within 14 days of purchase. After that, cancellation stops the next renewal.

## Statutory rights

Where you are a consumer, this policy does not limit your statutory cancellation or refund
rights, which apply in addition. Under GDPR-baseline consumer rules that includes a 14-day
withdrawal period; where you asked us to begin an assessment immediately and it has completed,
that right may be reduced or lost, and we tell you so before you confirm.

## How refunds are made

To the original payment method, via Stripe, within 10 business days of approval. We do not hold
your card details at any point.
