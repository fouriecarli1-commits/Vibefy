## Why this exists

Assessments are produced with AI assistance. AI output can be wrong, and a wrong finding
published about your application is a real harm. This policy is how you get that corrected. It
is free, it is available to every customer including free-tier customers, and using it never
counts against you.

## What you can appeal

- A specific finding you believe is factually wrong, mis-scoped or already remediated;
- A severity or confidence rating;
- An overall score or a certification decision;
- A refusal to assess;
- A badge suspension or revocation.

## How to appeal

Open an appeal from the report or badge in your console, stating the grounds. You will be asked
for at least a short explanation, because "I disagree" is not something we can act on. Attach
evidence if you have it.

## What we do

1. **Acknowledgement** within 2 business days.
2. **Review by a person who was not the original reviewer**, wherever staffing allows.
3. **Decision within 14 days** of acknowledgement. If we need longer we will say so, and why.

Outcomes: upheld (finding withdrawn or amended, score recomputed, badge reinstated), partially
upheld, or rejected with reasons.

## What happens to the record

If a finding is withdrawn, it is removed from the published report and marked withdrawn in the
audit log — the log is append-only, so the correction is added, never a deletion of the
original. Scores are recomputed against the **same rubric version** the assessment originally
used. If a badge was suspended in error, it is reinstated and the reinstatement is recorded as a
badge event.

## Corrections we initiate

If we discover an error ourselves, we correct it on the same terms and tell you, whether or not
the correction is in your favour. A score that was too generous is corrected as readily as one
that was too harsh.
