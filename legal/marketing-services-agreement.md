## 1. What this is

A separately branded, separately operated marketing service for app builders. It is not part of
the rating business, and buying it changes nothing about how an application is assessed.

## 2. The firewall

1. **Separate brand and separate codebase boundary.** Marketing services are not sold from the
   assessment console, and marketing staff have no access to assessment or review systems.
2. **No influence on scores.** The scoring function structurally cannot receive a marketing
   relationship as an input, and our build fails if anyone tries to add one. A test asserts that
   a marketing client and a free-tier customer with identical applications score identically.
3. **No influence on review.** Reviewers cannot see whether a customer is a marketing client,
   and must recuse themselves from any application in which they have an interest.
4. **No sequencing.** We will not offer marketing services in the same conversation as an
   assessment outcome, and we will not condition one on the other in either direction.
5. **Disclosure.** Every public surface showing a rating for a marketing client says so — the
   verification page and the directory listing.

## 3. What is offered

[To be specified at M8.] Whatever it is, it will not include: influencing a score, delaying a
suspension, obtaining a badge, preferential organic placement, or removing a finding.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice. Terminating marketing services has no effect on
an assessment, a score or a badge — in either direction.

## 5. Precedence

Where this Agreement and the Rating Methodology & Independence Policy conflict, the Independence
Policy prevails.
