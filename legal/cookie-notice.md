## What we set

| Cookie            | Purpose             | Type               | Lifetime           |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| `sb-*-auth-token` | Keeps you signed in | Strictly necessary | Session, refreshed |

That is the whole list. We set no analytics cookie, no advertising cookie, and nothing that
remembers a cookie choice — because there is nothing optional to choose. We do not share data
with advertising networks.

## If we ever set an optional cookie

We would say so here before setting it, not after. It would be **off until you turned it on**:
a banner offering "Accept" and "Reject" as equally prominent choices, no pre-ticked box, no
cookie wall, rejecting one click exactly like accepting. You could change your choice at any
time from the footer, and withdrawing would be as easy as giving.

Strictly necessary cookies are set without consent because the service cannot function without
them. We do not classify anything as necessary that is merely convenient for us.

## The badge on your site

When a visitor loads a page displaying a "Verified by VibefyCode" badge, their browser requests the
badge image from our servers. That request carries the visitor's IP address, user agent and the
referring origin. **We use it for one purpose: detecting badges displayed on a domain other
than the certified one, which is how forgery is caught.** It sets no cookie, builds no profile,
and is not used for analytics or advertising. Retention follows the badge-telemetry entry in
the Privacy Policy.

If you display the badge, you should mention this in your own cookie or privacy notice.
