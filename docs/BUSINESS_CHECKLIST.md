# Business checklist

Non-code work that must be finished before Vibefy takes a single paying customer.
Claude Code maintains this list; the founder ticks the boxes.

## Before the first paying customer

- [ ] **Corporate entity with limited liability formed.** Personal liability for a certification claim is not a risk worth carrying.
- [ ] **Professional indemnity / errors-and-omissions cover.** Essential the moment we certify anything. Confirm the policy covers AI-assisted assessment services specifically.
- [ ] **Cyber liability cover.** We hold assessment evidence and, for paid tiers, touch customer source code.
- [ ] **Counsel review of every document in `/legal`.** The drafts exist to make this review cheap and fast, not to replace it.
- [ ] Separate business banking, in the entity's name.
- [ ] Written incident response plan, including a 72-hour breach notification path.

## Before any brand spend

- [ ] **Trademark search for "Vibefy"** in the primary markets, classes **42** (software services) and **35** (business/advertising services).
- [ ] **Trademark search for the "Verified by Vibefy" badge mark**, same classes.
- [ ] Domain availability and acquisition: primary domain plus the `verify.` subdomain.
- [ ] App Store and Play Store developer-name availability check.
- [ ] Social handle availability check.
- [ ] **Record the outcome of all of the above in this file.** A trust mark we do not own is not a trust mark — the entire business rests on being the only party who can issue that badge.

## Before hiring anyone

- [ ] Contractor agreement template with full IP assignment.
- [ ] Confidentiality terms covering customer source code and assessment evidence.
- [ ] Reviewer conflict-of-interest declaration, required by the independence policy.
